This Privacy Policy explains how SweldoPH ByYags — Salary & Pay Calculator (Android package com.byyags.sweldoph; "SweldoPH," "the app"), developed by ByYags, handles information when you use the app.

1. Information you enter

SweldoPH lets you enter salary amounts, work hours, pay schedules, allowances, and related information to calculate estimated compensation and deductions. The app is designed to perform these calculations on your device. Saved calculation history and app preferences are designed to remain in local device storage.

ByYags does not require you to register an account to use the calculators. SweldoPH is not designed to send your salary entries or calculation history to ByYags servers. If you choose to share or copy a calculation using your device's share features, the information you choose to share is handled by the selected destination app or service.

Device-level backup or restore features, if enabled by your operating system and app configuration, may handle locally stored app data according to your device provider's settings and policies.

2. Advertising and information handled by Google

SweldoPH may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. When advertising is enabled, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share information such as your device's IP address (which can indicate approximate location), interactions with the app and advertisements, diagnostic or performance information, and device or advertising identifiers. Google uses this information for purposes that may include ad delivery, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention.

On supported Android versions, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may also use Android's Privacy Sandbox advertising APIs (such as Topics and Attribution Reporting), which you can manage in your device's Android ad privacy settings.

Advertising data is handled by Google under its policies and applicable consent settings. Learn more at Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required, SweldoPH will present advertising privacy choices through Google's consent-management tools. If the app offers a Privacy options control in Settings, you can use it to review or change applicable choices. You can also manage your device's advertising ID and ad privacy settings through Android settings.

3. How information is used

  • To calculate estimated salary, overtime, holiday pay, contributions, withholding tax, and related amounts.
  • To save preferences and calculation history locally, where you choose to use those features.
  • To provide and measure advertisements when advertising is enabled.
  • To support application stability, security, and prevention of abuse, where supported by integrated SDKs.

4. Sharing and third-party services

SweldoPH is not designed to disclose your entered salary figures to ByYags or to advertisers. If AdMob is enabled, Google receives information described in Section 2 through its advertising SDK. Data you deliberately share through your phone's sharing features is subject to the receiving service's privacy practices.

ByYags does not sell users' entered salary calculations or locally saved calculation histories.

5. Data retention and deletion

Calculation history and preferences stored by the app generally remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app, subject to device backup and restore behavior. Where the app provides a history-clear function, you can use it to remove saved history.

ByYags does not maintain a user account database for SweldoPH's offline calculation features. Data collected by Google through AdMob is retained and deleted according to Google's policies and applicable user controls. For privacy questions or requests, contact jeromimaru.jb@gmail.com.

6. Security

SweldoPH is designed to minimize collection by keeping calculator inputs and saved results on your device. The security of locally stored information also depends on your device security, Android configuration, and backups. Communications from integrated advertising services are managed by their SDKs and applicable transport-security protections.

7. Children

SweldoPH is a general-purpose salary calculator intended primarily for people seeking employment and payroll estimates. It is not specifically designed for children. The Google Play target-audience declaration and the app's advertising configuration must reflect the audience actually selected for distribution.

8. International use

SweldoPH focuses on Philippine payroll calculations but may be accessible from other countries. Where third-party advertising is enabled, Google's processing and associated privacy rights may vary depending on your region.

9. Changes to this policy

ByYags may update this policy when the app's functionality, advertising integrations, or applicable requirements change. An updated version will be published at this page with a revised effective date.
